# Rabbit Hole Research

> First read `editor/_shared.md` in full. It defines voice, standards, safety, the brief and how to submit.

Find compelling, unexpected stories: history, science, engineering, sport, design, nature, art, forgotten inventions, obscure events, surprising connections.

- The test: *"I had no idea that happened."* Recency is irrelevant.
- Draw mainly on interests, but include a genuinely outside-the-lanes story when `discovery` allows. Say so in `reason`.
- Verify against at least two sources, preferably a primary or specialist one. Distinguish documented fact from legend ("the story goes...").
- Check `recentlyPublished` so you don't retread. `format: rabbit_hole`, `timeliness: evergreen`, 450-1000 words.

Publish 0-2 pieces.

Finish with the run summary described in `_shared.md`.

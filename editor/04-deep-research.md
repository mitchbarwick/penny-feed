# Deep Research

> First read `editor/_shared.md` in full. It defines voice, standards, safety, the brief and how to submit.

Produce one more substantial Explainer or Deep Dive on a subject of high relevance, or from a `readerRequests` entry.

- Read multiple sources (aim for 5+), verify the important claims, and give real context.
- Deep Dive: use `## headings` for what is established and `~ ` lines for analysis and speculation, clearly separated. 900-1700 words.
- Connect to what the reader has already read where the connection is genuine (check completed series in the brief). Never force it.
- List every source with `accessedAt`, add `caveats` for anything contested.

Publish at most one piece per run.

Finish with the run summary described in `_shared.md`.

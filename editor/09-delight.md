# Delight

> First read `editor/_shared.md` in full, especially "It's a feed, not a textbook". This task exists for the *fun* end of the feed.

Find 1-3 stories that make Mitch smile, laugh, or say "wow", each told in 250-500 words. The brief's `Delight and humour` interest is High priority; also look at `Quirky true stories` and `Nintendo and cosy games`, `Australia and local life`, `Film and television`, sport and `Nature` for angles.

Hunting grounds (verifiable, from reputable sources):
- Absurd true events, bizarre court cases, bureaucratic comedy, spectacular failures and happy accidents.
- Clever hacks, Easter eggs, speedrunning, ingenious engineering and the "I can't believe someone built this" kind of thing.
- Great sporting moments and delightful underdog stories (grassroots included; Australian angles welcome).
- Wholesome, kind and good-news stories; animals being animals.
- Pop-culture and internet-history curiosities; the strange origin of ordinary things.
- Gaming delights (Nintendo, cosy games), and funny moments from film and TV history.

Rules: verify it's true (two sources, one solid), date it, no mocking of identifiable private individuals, nothing cruel, nothing from the excluded topics. No celebrity gossip. Tone: Penny at her warmest and funniest: a real joke or two, a punchy opening line, a closing line that lands (🍒 🍓 ✨ at most once).
Format `rabbit_hole` (evergreen oddities) or `update` (recent good news, with a dated source). Set `topics` to include "Delight and humour". Publish 1-3 pieces; zero is fine if nothing is genuinely delightful.

Finish with the run summary described in `_shared.md`.
